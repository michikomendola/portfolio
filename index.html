About me

I'm a Quality Assurance Tester from Metro Cebu with 6+ years of hands-on software testing experience and 10+ years of professional work. I collaborate with developers, product owners, and designers to turn requirements into testable coverage — then prove the product behaves the way users and regulators expect.

At AmwestEntertainment I test a live gambling platform: functional, regression, smoke, and exploratory testing, plus Cypress automation in TypeScript. I write reproducible Jira defects, support UAT and compliance documentation, and keep the automation suite healthy. Earlier I was a QA Specialist at Dynata and performed Business Objects internal QA at Teradyne, including SQL validation with TOAD for Oracle.

I'm ISTQB Foundation Level certified, a BSIT graduate of STI College Ormoc (GWA 1.59), and continuously sharpening automation through Cypress UI, API, and network-intercept coursework. I am looking for a full-time QA role where careful testing makes a measurable difference.
