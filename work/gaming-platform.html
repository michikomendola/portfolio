Live gambling platform

QA Tester · AmwestEntertainment, LLC · Manual, automation, and compliance-minded UAT

This role is the core of my recent QA practice: a production gambling site where incorrect balances, broken flows, or weak regression can cost money and trust. I work with developers, project managers, and UX designers in Agile ceremonies.

What I own

  • Review requirements and design docs, then write test cases with clear coverage.
  • Run functional, regression, smoke, and exploratory sessions on new releases.
  • File reproducible defects in Jira and verify fixes before sign-off.
  • Write and maintain Cypress (TypeScript) UI automation; debug flaky tests.
  • Support UAT and documentation needed for regulatory / compliance review.

How I think about risk

I bias coverage toward money movement, account state, session handling, and anything a player can retry under load. Exploratory charters sit beside scripted regression so we catch both known regressions and new product surprises.
